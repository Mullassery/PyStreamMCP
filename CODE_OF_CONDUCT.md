# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to
**mullassery@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated. This is a single-maintainer project, so there is no formal
committee — reports go directly to the maintainer.
